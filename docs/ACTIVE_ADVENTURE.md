# Active, player-controlled adventure — 0.2

**Presentation update:** [Living story 0.3](LIVING_STORY.md) supersedes the old
Journey layout and narrator budget described in this document. The mechanical
rules below remain, with public notice inspection and seeded spiritual sense added.
Portable saves are now v4 with v1/v2/v3 import support. [Persistent frontier 0.7](PERSISTENT_FRONTIER.md) extends the small map with saved roads and gathering tables.

This increment **extends** the existing `:core`, Compose screens, AndroidViewModel,
Room aggregate repository, DataStore preferences, character generator and save
codec. It does not replace the application or delete journal/save features.

## What existed and what changed

Previously working: creator, generated characters, inspection, a private journal,
independent slots, autosave/manual snapshots, backups, import/export. AI and combat
were interfaces, not implementations; God Mode was a persisted flag/banner, not an
editor. There was no AI provider profile to replace.

Now implemented:

- An active adventure screen with a fixed free-text composer, scene, time, health,
  energy, progression, nearby NPCs and authoritative action receipts.
- Optional convenience controls and navigation to Character, Cultivation,
  Inventory, Techniques, Quests, Map, NPCs, Events, Journal and Settings.
- A bounded connected world: Azure Cloud Village, Jade River Crossing, Silent Pine
  Mountain and discoverable Moonfall Ruins. Custom legacy starting locations gain
  a refuge connected to the village without renaming the player's origin.
- Persistent Mei, Elder Wen and Scout Ren, generated by the shared character
  framework with different roles, attributes, personalities, knowledge and goals.
- Travel, observations and named item/technique/NPC inspection; investigations,
  finite resource searches, dialogue memory, price negotiation, stock/currency
  transfers, two quests with one-time rewards, sect reputation and explicit entry.
- Single-opponent, turn-based wolf encounters and nonlethal NPC duels: attacks,
  known techniques, defending, dodging, positioning, pills, negotiation, retreat,
  injuries, victory/defeat and deterministic calculations.
- Optional instant activities, explicit breakthroughs, scheduled NPC/faction
  updates, and explicitly armed/claimed real-time activity banking.
- One configurable OpenAI-compatible chat/completions provider, encrypted key
  storage, connection test, bounded context, cancellation, and narration retry.

## Interaction and authority

The pipeline is:

1. Player submits their own words (up to 2,000 characters), or an explicit shortcut.
2. A local interpreter handles ordinary supported intents. Unknown wording goes
   to the configured AI, or to a bounded offline creative-attempt skill check.
3. Only allowlisted, typed `GameAction` values can reach the engine. AI cannot
   supply damage, attributes, inventory patches, God Mode or simulation settings.
4. Consequential externally interpreted proposals are shown for confirmation.
   Routine observe/investigate/creative-attempt proposals can resolve directly.
   This review prevents ambiguous model interpretation from silently choosing a
   duel, dialogue, trade, faction, activity or resource expenditure for the player.
5. The engine validates location, identity, encounter context, requirements,
   resources and resulting state. Rejection changes no mechanics/RNG/time.
6. Mechanics plus a uniquely identified receipt commit transactionally **before**
   any narration request. A repeated receipt ID cannot replay mechanics.
7. The AI narrates the committed facts. No response can mutate the campaign.
   Cancellation/failure retains the engine results. Retry narrates the latest
   receipt; it never repeats damage, purchases, rewards or elapsed time.

Player text is an attempt, not a declaration of success. Unsupported arbitrary
powers do not become new rules: an offline creative attempt may reveal information
or gain combat positioning, but never grants an asserted victory or realm. AI
interpretation expands wording, not the engine's capabilities. Brand-new mechanical
systems, items or techniques still require validated content implementation.

NPC responses are anchored to their stored knowledge/goal/personality, and their
actual encounters are recorded. Dialogue does not automatically grant affection,
obedience or promises. AI prose is labeled separately from authoritative results;
unsupported numeric tokens and common player-agency phrases are rejected. This is
not a proof that all natural-language contradictions can be detected. Inspect the
engine facts when prose is inconsistent; prose is never used as mechanical state.

## Mechanical rules in this increment

### Exploration, social systems and world time

Starter roads still cost two in-game hours. As of 0.7, other adjacent discovered roads use their persisted durations (generated routes: 1–4 hours). Investigating,
searching, speaking, trading and most combat turns cost one hour. Inspecting and
changing equipment do not advance time. Search opportunities are finite (three per
location); entering the guarded ruins courtyard starts a wolf encounter, whereas
surveying the outer wards can avoid combat. Ren's current location is inspectable.

Mei sells herbs (4 stones) and pills (12); successful bargaining reduces purchase
prices by one. She buys herbs/materials for 2 and pills for 6, subject to currency
and inventory. Stock and currency actually transfer. Quest rewards can be claimed
once. Azure Cloud entry requires visiting Wen, stage 2 or higher and 5 reputation,
unless an explicitly enabled sandbox save bypasses requirements.

No timer runs the main world in the background. Time advances through player
commands. Background modes affect scheduled NPC/faction simulation:

- **Paused:** schedules are frozen; explicit player actions still take world time.
- **Normal:** schedule time follows action time.
- **Accelerated:** three schedule hours per action hour; player action costs do not
  change. Both deltas appear in calculation logs.

Each scheduled day supplies 5 sect resources and alternates Ren's patrol between
mountain and crossing. Every third day replenishes Mei's existing stock by two.
These are bounded scheduled developments, not arbitrary interruptions. No mode
accepts quests, declares wars, chooses dialogue or travels on the player's behalf.
Events retain the most recent 200 entries. The player ages per 365 elapsed world
days if lifespan rules are enabled; natural-death/succession mechanics are not yet
implemented and should not be inferred from the lifespan field.

### Combat

Combat uses actual character records, stored RNG state and the same hit/damage
function for both sides. Strength or spiritual power, agility, sense, luck,
constitution, soul strength, realm/stage, technique cost/mastery, equipped
weapon/artifact/armor, positioning, injury and elemental resistance matter.
Comprehension and talent affect progression/creative checks rather than being
unused combat-power labels.

The log exposes hit rolls, thresholds, offense, defense, criticals, mitigation and
applied damage. Enemy decisions use an available damage technique every third turn
when requirements/energy allow it. Gentle/Harsh difficulty applies an explicit
0.8/1.2 multiplier to incoming enemy damage; Standard is 1.0. This is a documented
game-mode modifier, not a hidden stat rewrite.

NPC duels are nonlethal and do not permanently damage NPC health records. Opponent
turn state is persisted inside the encounter. A first victory over a given opponent
awards 12 stones and up to 8 cultivation progress; repeat victories do not farm
those rewards. Defeat leaves the player at the same location with 1 health and a
35% attack penalty, recoverable through chosen rest/meditation. No forced travel or
player dialogue occurs. Retreat success is rolled; a failed retreat allows a counterattack.

### Optional activities

Each request simulates **1–24 in-game hours instantly**, with a preflight estimate
and persisted actual results. No real-world wait or payment gate is imposed.

- **Cultivation:** talent/comprehension × method × configured speed × spring bonus;
  optional one herb/hour gives 50% extra progress. Focused Meridian Cycle is 35%
  faster than Still Water, but costs 3 energy/hour. Progress caps at 100%.
- **Meditation:** restores health/energy and reduces injury, capped at maxima.
- **Technique practice:** trains all known techniques and technique-mastery stats;
  individual technique mastery caps at 100%.
- **Body training:** adds strength per hour within structural stat limits.
- **Alchemy:** 2 herbs + 3 stones/hour makes a pill restoring 40 health.
- **Crafting:** 2 ore + 2 stones/hour makes armor with 5 defensive power.

Breakthrough is a separate explicit action. The UI previews the same chance used
by the engine and the stone cost. Failure consumes the cost, leaves 75% progress,
adds injury and costs health without killing the player. Realm ascent records a
tribulation when not skipped and increases maximum lifespan according to the
configured hierarchy. Custom realm references are preserved.

Optional real-time banking is **off by default**. Arming a plan records a timestamp,
activity, method and herb choice. Whole elapsed real-time hours can later be
claimed explicitly, capped at 24; the timer measures time while armed, including
foreground time, rather than running a background worker. Claims check resources
and update the checkpoint atomically. Excess beyond 24 hours and fractional hours
are discarded on claim. No repeated claim of the same interval is possible. A
clock moved backwards yields no available time. Device clock manipulation is not
prevented in this single-player game. Claims are blocked during combat.

## AI settings, privacy and failure recovery

Settings accepts the **complete HTTPS chat/completions URL**, model, API key and
sampling temperature. One profile is supported; replacing providers does not
change saves. The API key is encrypted with AES-GCM using Android Keystore and
stored separately from campaigns. It is not placed in exports, UI saved-state
bundles or model messages. Redirects are disabled to avoid forwarding credentials
to another host. No real API keys are checked into the repository.

Only the submitted intention, observed scene/recorded interaction memories, bounded target
IDs, a compact player summary and authoritative facts are sent. Private journal
entries and hidden traits are excluded. Provider calls are opt-in and may incur
provider charges. **Test connection** sends no campaign information.

Requests have timeouts, bounded responses, cancellation and explicit retry only.
Authentication, rate-limit, model/endpoint, connection, oversized and malformed
responses produce safe errors without dumping provider bodies or credentials.
If interpretation fails, nothing mechanical has run and the draft remains. If
narration fails, mechanics are already saved. Local play remains available.
Tests use a local mock HTTP server; no real paid provider/model account has been
used for live integration verification.

## Persistence and upgrading from the tested 0.1 APK

**Export your existing campaign before installing a new CI debug APK.** Different
CI runners may use different debug signing keys. Android may require uninstalling
before a differently signed build can be installed, which deletes private saves.
Export first, install, then import your `.xianxia.json`. Never uninstall to fix a
signature error without an external copy of your saves.

Portable format v2 adds an `adventure` aggregate. v1 decoding preserves identity,
attributes, roots, traits, techniques, inventory, settings, journal, timestamps
and RNG. On activation, missing adventure state is initialized deterministically
and written through the existing repository with a pre-overwrite backup. v2
encounters, receipts, NPC memories, stock, quests, events, simulation clocks and
idle checkpoints all round-trip. There is no destructive Room schema migration:
SQL schema v1 still stores the complete validated aggregate transactionally.

Every adventure action (including a recorded rejection) commits immediately,
even if private-journal autosave is off. It also commits any pending journal/name
edits in the active working copy. Those existing manual/autosave controls still
apply to journal/name-only edits. Save snapshots, independent slots, rename,
duplicate, import/export, restore and delete remain available. Existing God Mode
flags remain explicit: in this slice they protect from enemy damage and bypass
activity/breakthrough resource/realm requirements. They cannot be enabled by AI.

## Deliberately incomplete

This is a playable bounded adventure, **not the full master-spec game**. Still
outstanding: large procedural worlds, arbitrary generated mechanical content,
group battles/tournaments/sect wars, complete sect management, complex crafting
recipes/upgrades, multiple provider profiles, robust semantic verification of all
AI prose, romance/descendants, mod packages, the graphical God Mode/world editor,
and production signing. These are not represented by fake working buttons.
Physical-phone UI/SAF/accessibility checks and a live provider test remain separate
from emulator and mock-provider verification.
